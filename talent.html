Compensation & Payment Structure

Transparent information about how we work with our team members and how you'll be compensated.

Commission-Based Positions

Many of our positions are commission-based, meaning your income is directly tied to your performance and results. This structure rewards high performers and provides unlimited earning potential.

How It Works:

  • Base commission rates vary by role and performance tier
  • Commission is calculated on closed deals, completed projects, or achieved milestones
  • Higher performers earn higher commission percentages
  • No cap on earnings - the more you deliver, the more you earn

1099 Independent Contractor Status

Team members at Recipe Labs work as independent contractors (1099), not employees. This provides flexibility and autonomy in how you work.

What This Means:

  • You operate as an independent business entity
  • Flexible work schedule and location
  • You're responsible for your own taxes (see Tax Information below)
  • No traditional benefits (health insurance, PTO, etc.)
  • You can work with other clients/companies simultaneously

How We Pay

We use modern payment systems to ensure fast, reliable payments for all our contractors.

Payment Methods:

  • Direct Deposit (ACH): Fastest method, typically 1-2 business days
  • Wire Transfer: Available for larger payments, same-day processing
  • PayPal: Available upon request
  • Check: Traditional check mailing available

Payment Schedule:

  • Commission payments: Processed within 7-14 days of deal closure/project completion
  • Monthly retainer positions: Paid on the 1st and 15th of each month
  • All payments include detailed invoices for your records

Tax Information & Forms

As a 1099 independent contractor, you'll receive tax forms and need to handle your own tax obligations.

Tax Forms You'll Receive:

  • Form 1099-NEC: Issued if you earn $600 or more in a calendar year
  • Forms are mailed by January 31st of the following year
  • Digital copies available in your contractor portal

Your Tax Responsibilities:

  • You're responsible for paying your own income taxes
  • Estimated quarterly tax payments may be required
  • Self-employment tax applies (Social Security & Medicare)
  • Keep detailed records of all business expenses
  • Consider consulting a tax professional for guidance

Deductions You May Qualify For:

  • Home office expenses (if you work from home)
  • Business equipment and software
  • Professional development and training
  • Internet, phone, and utilities (business portion)
  • Travel and meal expenses (when applicable)

Income Potential

Commission-based roles offer significant earning potential for motivated individuals.

Earning Examples (Varies by Role):

  • Entry Level: $30,000 - $50,000 annually (commission-based)
  • Mid Level: $50,000 - $100,000 annually (commission-based)
  • Senior Level: $100,000+ annually (commission-based, uncapped)
  • Performance bonuses and tier multipliers available
  • Top performers can exceed $150,000+ annually

Note: Income figures are estimates and actual earnings depend on individual performance, role, and market conditions. No income is guaranteed.

Important Information

  • All positions are 1099 independent contractor roles (not W-2 employees)
  • No traditional employee benefits (health insurance, retirement plans, PTO)
  • You must have or obtain your own business insurance if required
  • You're responsible for your own equipment and tools
  • Contract terms and commission structures are outlined in your independent contractor agreement
  • We recommend consulting with a tax professional before starting
