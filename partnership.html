Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the Selected Partner Program:

How do I apply for the Selected Partner Program?

Start by scheduling a consultation call. We'll discuss your organization's needs, goals, and how Recipe Labs can help. If there's a mutual fit, we'll extend an invitation to the Selected Partner Program.

What happens if pricing needs to be adjusted after the contract period?

We provide 60 days advance notice for any pricing adjustments, with transparent rationale and mutual discussion. Adjustments are designed to ensure continued alignment with your growth and our ability to deliver exceptional value. Most partners see pricing remain stable or adjust only with significant service expansions.

What's included in the "enterprise infrastructure"?

Complete backend automated systems, workflow integration (existing or new), custom database development, real-time dashboard notification centers, AI-powered virtual company assistants, and ongoing management and support. Everything is customized to your specific needs.

How long does implementation take?

Most partners see initial systems deployed within 30-60 days, with full implementation complete within 90-120 days. We prioritize quick wins that deliver immediate value while building toward comprehensive automation.

Do you work with organizations outside New England?

Yes! While we're based in Hartford, CT, we serve organizations across New England and the greater online general public. Our virtual infrastructure and remote collaboration capabilities allow us to work effectively with partners anywhere.
