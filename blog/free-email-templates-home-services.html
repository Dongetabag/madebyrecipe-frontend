The Home Service Business's Customer Retention Challenge

Home service businesses face unique marketing challenges:

  • One-time customers: Most customers only call when something breaks

  • High competition: Multiple contractors competing for the same jobs
  • Referral generation: Happy customers are your best source of new business
  • Maintenance programs: Converting one-time customers into maintenance plan subscribers

Email marketing solves all of these—but only if your emails look professional and drive action.

Why Email Marketing Works for Home Services

Email is the most effective channel for home service marketing because:
